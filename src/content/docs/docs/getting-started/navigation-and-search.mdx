BuildWorkPro has two ways to get anywhere: the sidebar for browsing and the command palette for jumping straight to a record.

![The command palette open over the dashboard with "chiller" typed, listing matching site logs, a lead, and projects, each labeled with its record type](/docs-screenshots/getting-started/command-palette-search.png)

## The sidebar

The dark navy sidebar down the left edge lists every module by name, grouped the way a subcontractor's work flows:

- **Dashboard** -- your home screen.
- **Sell** -- Pipeline, Contacts, and Bids.
- **Build** -- Projects, Calendar, Site Logs, and Time.
- **Get Paid** -- Change Orders, Pay Apps, and Invoices.
- **Library** -- Products, Documents, Reports, and Activity.
- **Settings** -- account and company settings, at the bottom.

Badges on a module flag work waiting on you -- bids needing follow-up, unreviewed site logs, pending change orders and pay apps, overdue invoices. The **+ New** button at the top creates a contact, lead, project, bid, or invoice from anywhere, and a **Recent** list keeps the records you opened last, with a star to pin the ones you return to.

Click **Collapse** at the bottom, or press <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd> on Windows or Linux), to shrink the sidebar to a narrow rail of labeled icons.

What you see depends on your role. Field Crew, for example, does not see Bids or billing.

<Aside type="note">
  Notifications are not in the sidebar. Open them from the bell icon in the top header.
</Aside>

On a phone, the sidebar becomes a bottom bar -- Dashboard, Pipeline, Bids, Projects, and Time for office roles; Dashboard, Projects, Site Logs, and Time for Field Crew -- plus a **More** button for everything else.

## The command palette

The command palette is the fastest way to find a specific record. Press <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on Windows or Linux. You can also click the search box in the header.

Start typing and the palette searches across your whole workspace:

Contacts, Leads, Projects, Bids, Change Orders, Pay Apps, Invoices, Site Logs, Documents, Products, Tasks, and CSV Import Templates.

Select a result to jump straight to it. Before you type, the palette also lists quick actions and your recent searches.

<Aside type="tip">
  Narrow a search to one record type with a `type:` prefix. For example, `type:bid oak valley` searches only bids. Aliases include `type:contact`, `type:project`, `type:co` for change orders, and `type:payapp` for pay apps.
</Aside>

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> | Open the command palette |
| <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> | Save the open form |
| <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd> | Collapse or expand the sidebar |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Close a dialog, menu, or inline editor |
