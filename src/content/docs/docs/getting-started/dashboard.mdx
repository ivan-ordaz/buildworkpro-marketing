The dashboard is your home screen. It opens whenever you sign in and starts with what needs you today, then your money, then your key numbers. A time-based greeting at the top welcomes you by name, and a period selector (**Year to Date** by default) sets the window for the numbers and charts.

![The BuildWorkPro dashboard with the attention banner, the Needs you today list of follow-ups sorted by money at stake, and the money strip showing overdue AR, outstanding, collected in 30 days, pay apps, and pending change orders](/docs-screenshots/getting-started/dashboard.png)

## Needs you today

The **Needs you today** list is your action queue, sorted by money at stake. Each row has its next step one click away -- **Send follow-up** on a quiet bid, **Review bid** on one about to expire, **Send reminder** on an overdue invoice, **Draft pay app**, **Review** an unreviewed site log, or **Check in** on a jobsite that has gone silent. The list shows the top items; **Show all** expands it, and the header collapses it.

When something needs attention -- overdue projects or bids needing follow-up -- a banner above the list counts them and links straight to them. You can dismiss it.

## Your money

Two always-on panels track cash:

- **The money strip** -- **Overdue AR**, **Outstanding**, **Collected 30d**, **Pay apps** awaiting approval, and **Pending COs**.
- **Money on the books** -- contract, billed, and paid across every live project that is not fully paid.

Both hide for roles that cannot see billing.

## KPI cards

A row of stat cards tracks your key numbers. By default you see four:

- **Pipeline Value** -- the total value of open leads.
- **Win Rate** -- the share of decided bids you have won.
- **Active Projects** -- projects currently in progress.
- **Pending Bids** -- bids still in draft or sent.

Each card links to a filtered list, so clicking **Active Projects** opens your in-progress projects. Other available cards include Contract Value, Planning, Overdue, Completed, Won Leads (est.), and Contacts.

<Aside type="tip">
  The dashboard is yours to arrange. Click **Customize** to show or hide cards and widgets and drag them into the order you want.
</Aside>

## Quick actions

Quick-action buttons start the most common tasks without leaving the dashboard:

- **Add Contact** -- opens the new-contact form.
- **Add Lead** -- opens the new-lead form.
- **Create Bid** -- starts a new bid.
- **New Project** -- opens the new-project form.

You only see the actions your role allows. A Viewer, who cannot create records, sees none of them.

## Widgets

The rest of the dashboard is a grid of widgets you can rearrange from the **Customize** panel. They include:

- **Revenue History** -- revenue over time.
- **This Week Summary** -- what is due and happening this week.
- **Pipeline & Bids** -- open pipeline and bid activity.
- **Project Status** -- a breakdown of projects by status.
- **Win / Loss** -- decided bids, won versus lost.
- **Upcoming Deadlines**, **At Risk**, **Project Map**, **Site Log Activity**, **Projects by Type**, and **Crew Today**.

## Getting started

A **Your first 14 days** checklist walks you through the core loop -- create your first bid, send it, convert the win to a project, bill it, bring your crew in, and connect QuickBooks or Google. On a brand-new workspace it leads the dashboard; once you have data it sits below the quick actions. Dismiss it when you are done -- reopen it anytime from **Getting started** in your account menu.
