The **Pricing** tab controls how your costs become a sell price. It sets the margin, commission, overhead, and sales tax applied to the bid.

![The Pricing tab of a bid, showing the Rate Settings card with margin, commission, company overhead, and sales tax, and the Live Preview panel](/docs-screenshots/bids/bid-rates-tab.png)

## Rate settings

Open the **Pricing** tab. Under **Profit & Overhead**, the **Rate Settings** card has three percentages, each expressed as a percentage of the selling price -- not a markup on cost (see [markup vs. margin](/blog/construction-markup-vs-margin/) for why that matters):

- **Margin** -- your profit margin.
- **Commission** -- sales commission.
- **Company OH** -- company overhead for general business expenses.

The **Live Preview** panel beside it shows how the rates turn your material and labor costs into the customer total as you type. Changes save automatically. Click **Reset to Defaults** to return to your organization's default rates.

## Sales tax

**Sales tax** on the same card is the rate every line inherits. A line, or one of its cost items, can set its own rate instead -- or 0% to exempt it -- and its own rate replaces the bid's; the two are never added together. Cost items also carry a **Purchase tax (cost)** rate on what you pay -- see [line items](/docs/bids/line-items/).

## Display options

The **Display Options** card has:

- **Round to whole dollars** -- show customer-facing totals on this bid without cents. It starts from your company setting.
- A link to the **Proposal** tab, where you choose how much your customer sees on the bid document: **Total only**, **Line items**, or a **Full breakdown** that lists each line's materials and labor (without your costs).

## Set defaults for new bids

To pre-fill these rates on every new bid, set your organization defaults.

<Steps>

1. **Open bid defaults.** Go to **Settings -> Bid & Estimate Defaults**.

2. **Edit Default Bid Rates.** In the **Default Bid Rates** card, set **Margin**, **Commission**, and **Company OH**. New bids start from these values.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  **Role required:** Company Admin to change the defaults. Per-bid rates can be adjusted by anyone who can edit the bid.
</Aside>
