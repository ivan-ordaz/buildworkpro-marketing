## Send the bid

<Steps>

1. **Review the bid.** Open the bid and check the line items, rates, and terms.

2. **Open the send dialog.** Click **Send Bid**.

3. **Compose the email.** Choose an **Email Template** if you have one, set the **To** recipient (use **Add Cc** or **Add Bcc** as needed), and edit the **Subject** and **Message**. The bid PDF attaches automatically.

4. **Send.** Click **Preview** to check the email, then click **Send Bid**.

</Steps>

The bid moves to **Sent** status.

## Track bid status

A bid has five statuses:

- **Draft** -- still being prepared.
- **Sent** -- delivered to the client.
- **Accepted** -- the client agreed.
- **Rejected** -- the client declined.
- **Expired** -- past the **Valid Until** date with no decision.

When a client responds, click **Mark Accepted**, enter who accepted it, and confirm with **Accept Bid** -- the accepted bid locks for editing. If they decline, click **Mark Rejected**. You can see every bid's status at a glance on the Bids list.

## Convert a won bid to a project

Once a bid is **Accepted**, turn it into a project.

<Steps>

1. **Start the conversion.** Open the accepted bid and click **Convert to Project**.

2. **Name it and choose a strategy.** The **Project name** carries over from the bid. Pick a **Conversion Strategy**:
   - **Phases from lines** -- each bid line item becomes a project phase.
   - **One phase, tasks from line items** -- each bid line item becomes a project task.
   - **Template** -- apply a predefined project template and map line items to its phases.
   - **Start blank** -- no phases or tasks; start from scratch.

   The **Preview** shows what will be created. Optionally set a start date and an expected end date.

3. **Convert.** Click **Convert to Project**.

</Steps>

The new project links back to the original bid, and its line items can seed the schedule of values for [pay applications](/docs/pay-apps/creating/).

<Aside type="tip">
  You can also click **Create Invoice** on an accepted bid to bill from it directly.
</Aside>
