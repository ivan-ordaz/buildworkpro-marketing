**Company profile** holds your company's identity -- the details shown on bids, invoices, and every document you send to customers. Open it from **Settings -> Company profile**, under **Company** in the settings menu.

![Company profile settings showing the company name, slug, industry, company size, phone, address, and logo fields](/docs-screenshots/settings/organization-settings.png)

## Company details

The **Company Profile** card holds:

- **Company Name** -- your company's name as it appears in the app and on documents.
- **Slug** -- a read-only short identifier for your workspace.
- **Industry** and **Company Size**.
- **Phone**, **Address**, **Business Email**, **Website**, and **License Number** -- the contact details printed on your documents.
- **Company Logo** -- upload a PNG, JPG, or WebP up to 2 MB. Horizontal and square logos both work.
- **Tagline** -- a short line shown with your company name.

<Aside type="note">
  **Role required:** Company Admin. Company profile is only available to Company Admins.
</Aside>

## Team management

Inviting teammates, editing roles, and deactivating accounts happen in **Settings -> Members & roles**. See [team and roles](/docs/settings/team-roles/) for the full role matrix and the invite flow.

## Where document styling and defaults live

The look of your documents -- style, accent color, and footer -- plus numbering, bid rates, templates, product categories, and pipeline stages live in the **Sales & Documents** group of the settings menu. See [sales and document settings](/docs/settings/workspace-defaults/) to set them.
