**Import & Export** brings records in from CSV files and exports your data back out. Open it from **Settings -> Import & Export**, under **Data & Developer** in the settings menu.

![Import & Export settings showing the Import Data card with four guided CSV importers and the Export Data card with filtered CSV exports](/docs-screenshots/settings/data-management.png)

<Aside type="note">
  **Role required:** Company Admin. Import & Export is only available to Company Admins.
</Aside>

## Import data

The **Import Data** card runs guided CSV wizards for:

- **Contacts**
- **Products**
- **Leads**
- **Projects**

<Steps>

1. **Pick a type.** Click **Open importer** on the type you are importing. Click **Sample CSV** first if you need the column layout.

2. **Upload your file.** Select your CSV and map its columns to BuildWorkPro fields.

3. **Import.** Review the preview and run the import.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
  For importing vendor price lists into a bid, use the [CSV Import Templates](/docs/documents/csv-import-templates/) feature instead -- it maps spreadsheet columns to bid line items.
</Aside>

## Export data

The **Export Data** card downloads your records as CSV -- **Export Contacts**, **Export Products**, **Export Leads**, or **Export Projects**. Each export takes a date range and filters (contact type, category, pipeline stage, project status, and so on), so you export just the rows you need.

## Starter templates and catalog

Below the import and export cards:

- **Starter Templates** -- add ready-made templates for your trade: a starter estimate and a standard project plan. Templates you already have are never changed.
- **Starter Catalog** -- add a ready-made product catalog for your trade with example pricing. Removing it later keeps anything you edited or used on a bid.
