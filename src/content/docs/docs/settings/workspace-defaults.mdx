The defaults that shape your bids and documents live in the **Sales & Documents** group of the settings menu: **Document Setup**, **Bid & Estimate Defaults**, **Templates**, **Product Catalog**, and **Lead Pipeline**. Open any of them from **Settings**.

![Document Setup settings showing the document style choices, accent color, footer, defaults for new bids, and a live preview of a proposal](/docs-screenshots/settings/workspace-defaults.png)

<Aside type="note">
  **Role required:** Company Admin for Document Setup, Bid & Estimate Defaults, Product Catalog, and Lead Pipeline. Managers can also reach **Templates** for the email templates, bid terms, and CSV import templates they manage.
</Aside>

## Document Setup

How every generated document looks -- proposals, change orders, invoices, pay applications, and daily reports -- and how it is numbered:

- **Document style** -- **Ledger**, **Modern**, or **Marquee**.
- **Accent Color** and **Footer** text.
- **Defaults for new bids** -- show the customer **Total only**, **Line items**, or the **Full breakdown**, and whether quantities and unit prices print.
- **Currency Display** -- round customer-facing amounts on bids and change orders to whole dollars.
- **Document Numbering** -- a **Prefix**, **Starting number**, and zero-padding for bids, projects, and invoices.

A **Live Preview** shows a sample proposal as you change the style. Your company name, logo, tagline, and contact details come from [Company profile](/docs/settings/organization/).

## Bid & Estimate Defaults

Defaults applied to every new bid:

- **Default Bid Rates** -- **Margin**, **Commission**, and **Company OH**, pre-filled on each new estimate.
- **Bid Validity & Terms** -- the default validity in days, the estimator SLA (how many days a draft bid without a due date can sit before the board warns you), and the default terms and conditions text.

## Templates

Reusable content, on four tabs:

- **Email** -- email templates for bids, change orders, pay apps, and invoices, used by the send dialogs.
- **Bid terms** -- **Payment Terms** and **Terms & Conditions** clause libraries to apply on a bid.
- **CSV import** -- [CSV import templates](/docs/documents/csv-import-templates/) that map a supplier spreadsheet into a bid.
- **Project** -- reusable phase-and-task structures. See [project templates](/docs/projects/templates/).

## Product Catalog

**Product Categories** group your [product catalog](/docs/products/catalog/), including parent and child categories. **Default Tax Rates for New Products** set the purchase and sales tax pre-filled on new products, per category.

## Lead Pipeline

**Lead Pipeline Stages** -- add, rename, reorder, and color your sales stages, and mark which stages count as Won or Lost. These stages drive the [CRM pipeline board](/docs/crm/pipeline/).

<Aside type="tip">
  Your own preferences -- restarting the onboarding tour and the keyboard shortcuts list -- are under **Settings -> Preferences**.
</Aside>
