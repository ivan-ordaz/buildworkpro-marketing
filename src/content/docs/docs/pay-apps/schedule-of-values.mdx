## The SOV table

On a pay app's detail page, the **Schedule of Values** card lists your line items. The columns are:

- **Item** -- the item number.
- **Description** -- what the line covers.
- **Source** -- the bid line or change order the row came from, or *Not linked*.
- **Scheduled Value** -- the contract amount for the line.
- **Previous Apps** -- completed and billed in prior pay apps.
- **This Period** -- completed in the current period.
- **% Complete** -- how far along the line is.
- **Materials Stored** -- materials on site but not yet installed.
- **Total to Date** -- the sum of previous, this period, and materials stored.

A **Grand Total** row sums every line.

## Bill the period

On a draft pay app, progress is entered right in the grid -- there is no dialog per line.

<Steps>

1. **Enter progress.** For each line, type the **This Period** amount, or type a **% Complete** and This Period is calculated for you. Enter moves down the column. The row's **Bill remaining** button fills a line to 100%.

2. **Add stored materials.** Enter **Materials Stored** for anything delivered but not yet installed.

3. **Save.** Click **Save N changes** (or **Discard**). Pay apps never auto-save. The payment summary recalculates retainage and the current payment due.

</Steps>

## Add a line item

<Steps>

1. **Add a line.** Click **Add Line Item**.

2. **Describe it.** Enter an **Item Number**, a **Description**, and the **Scheduled Value**.

3. **Bill it.** The new line joins the grid -- report its progress like any other line and **Save**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  **Previous Apps** is carried forward from the prior pay app and cannot be edited here. It locks once an earlier pay app exists for the project.
</Aside>

## How the SOV is seeded

The SOV is not imported with a button. On the first pay app, BuildWorkPro seeds one line per line item of the project's accepted bid -- each valued at the price the customer saw, and labeled with the bid line it came from in **Source** -- plus any pending approved change orders, and the **Original Contract Sum** prefills from that bid. A project with no accepted bid starts with an empty SOV for manual entry. Each later pay app carries forward the lines and previous-period totals from the one before it. Add or adjust lines with **Add Line Item** as the job evolves.
