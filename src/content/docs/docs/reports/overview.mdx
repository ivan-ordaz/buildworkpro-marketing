**Reports** opens on the **Business review** -- one page that answers how the business is doing for a period you pick. Each section links to a full report with the rows behind the numbers. Open it from **Reports** in the sidebar.

![The Business review for year to date, with Sold, Billed, Collected, and Owed to you cards above the billed-vs-collected chart, the who-owes-you list, and the sales section](/docs-screenshots/reports/reports.png)

<Aside type="note">
  **Role required:** Company Admin, Manager, Member, or Viewer can view reports. Field Crew has no access. Exporting requires Member or above.
</Aside>

## Pick a period

The period selector at the top right scopes everything -- **This Week** (the default), **This Month**, **This Quarter**, **Year to Date**, **Last Month**, **Last Quarter**, **Last Year**, **All Time**, or a **Custom range**. Turn on **vs prior period** to compare each figure with the period before it.

## The Business review

Four cards lead the page:

- **Sold** -- accepted bids, with the count won and the win rate.
- **Billed** -- invoices plus pay apps.
- **Collected** -- cash received, and the share of billed it represents.
- **Owed to you** -- open receivables as of today.

Below them, three sections:

- **Money** -- billed versus collected over trailing periods, and **Who owes you**, by customer.
- **Sales** -- bids sent versus won (win rate counts decided bids, with expired bids as lost), and the pipeline for the period: open pipeline value, new leads, bids sent, new contacts, and leads gone quiet.
- **Work in progress** -- backlog (contract versus billed on live projects) and schedule.

## Reports

Each section links to its full report, also listed under **Reports** in the sidebar:

- **AR aging** and **Billed vs collected** -- money.
- **Bid performance** and **Pipeline by source** -- sales.
- **WIP / backlog** -- work.

A report page has the same period selector, summary figures at the top, and a table of the rows behind them. Save a filter and period combination with **Save current view**, and use **Export -> Export as CSV** to download the table.
